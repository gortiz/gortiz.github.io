Privacy policy

Last update: October 2026.

This policy applies to the services on gortizja.com that use a Google account. These services are personal and only for invited people. They are not a public product.

Sign-in with Google

Some pages require a sign-in with a Google account. The sign-in reads your email address and your name. The email address is used only to find out if you have access. A session cookie keeps you signed in until the session expires.

Google Drive

Some services can store their own files in the Google Drive of the account that authorizes them. They can see only the files that they created. They cannot read other files in Google Drive.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share, or give your data to other persons or companies.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or for analytics.
  • We do not use Google user data to train AI or machine learning models.

The use of information received from Google APIs obeys the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

To remove the access of these services to your Google account, go to Third-party apps with account access. To ask for the deletion of your session data, open an issue in github.com/gortiz/gortiz.github.io.
