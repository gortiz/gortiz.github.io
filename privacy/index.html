Privacy policy

Last update: October 2026.

This policy applies to the Google sign-in and the Google Drive access that the services on gortizja.com use. These services are personal. They are for Gonzalo Ortiz and a small number of people that he invites. They are not a public product.

Sign-in with Google

Some pages on gortizja.com require a sign-in with a Google account. The sign-in reads your email address and your name. The server uses your email address only to find out if you have access. It keeps a session cookie until you sign out or the session expires. Only invited email addresses get access.

Google Drive

The backup of the home server writes encrypted files to the Google Drive of Gonzalo Ortiz. It uses the drive.file permission. With this permission, it can see only the files that it created. It cannot read other files in Google Drive. This access applies only to the account of Gonzalo Ortiz.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share, or give your data to other persons or companies.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or for analytics.
  • We do not use Google user data to train AI or machine learning models.

The use of information received from Google APIs obeys the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

To remove the access of these services to your Google account, go to Third-party apps with account access. To ask for the deletion of your session data, open an issue in github.com/gortiz/gortiz.github.io.
